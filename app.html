Edge score
Overall trading edge quality
What is Edge Score?
A transparent 0–100 diagnostic combining win rate, realised reward:risk, strategy-tag coverage, calm-state win rate when available, and the share of profitable weeks. Missing optional inputs are excluded rather than scored as zero.
Use this as a journaling diagnostic, not proof of a statistically durable edge.

What is P&L by time?
A heatmap showing your profitability broken down by day of week and time of day. Green cells = profitable windows. Red cells = losing windows. Darker colour = stronger signal.
Compare windows only after recording enough trades with actual entry times.

Behavioural patterns
Psychology impact on P&L
What are behavioural patterns?
Compares your logged emotions, strategy tags, and trading frequency with observed P&L. It reports associations in your journal without inferring intent or a psychological diagnosis.
🎯 The goal is to find what mental states make you profitable — then recreate them daily.
Early signal
Setup quality
Performance by logged setup
What is Setup quality?
Ranks every strategy/setup you trade by expectancy — the average amount you make per trade on that setup. Combines win rate and average win/loss size into one number. Positive = profitable setup. Negative = losing setup.
🎯 Scale up setups with positive expectancy. Pause or study setups with negative expectancy.

Drawdown DNA
What causes your losing streaks
What is Drawdown DNA?
Analyses the sequence of events that leads to your worst losing periods. Identifies trigger trades, escalation patterns and the point of maximum damage — so you can break the cycle before it starts.
🎯 Most drawdowns follow the same pattern. Knowing yours lets you stop it at step 1.

Bootstrap simulation
Range of monthly outcomes
What is the bootstrap simulation?
Resamples your recorded P&L to show a range of possible monthly outcomes if future trades resemble this sample. It reports simulated positive-month frequency and downside percentiles; it does not prove an edge.
Treat small samples and changing market regimes cautiously.
