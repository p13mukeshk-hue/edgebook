Key insight
Edge score
Overall trading edge quality
What is Edge Score?
A single number (0–100) that summarises the overall quality of your trading edge. It combines 5 sub-scores: win rate, R:R ratio, discipline (how often you trade with a strategy), emotional control (win rate when calm), and consistency.
🎯 Above 60 = real edge. Below 40 = focus on one setup until it's consistent.

Day × time performance
What is P&L by time?
A heatmap showing your profitability broken down by day of week and time of day. Green cells = profitable windows. Red cells = losing windows. Darker colour = stronger signal.
🎯 Stop trading during your red time windows. Double down during your green ones.

Behavioural patterns
Psychology impact on P&L
What are behavioural patterns?
Cross-references your psychology data (emotions, mood, rule compliance) with your P&L to detect patterns. Identifies revenge trading, overtrading, and the P&L impact of your emotional state.
🎯 The goal is to find what mental states make you profitable — then recreate them daily.
Early signal
Setup quality
Performance by strategy
What is Setup quality?
Ranks every strategy/setup you trade by expectancy — the average amount you make per trade on that setup. Combines win rate and average win/loss size into one number. Positive = profitable setup. Negative = losing setup.
🎯 Scale up setups with positive expectancy. Pause or study setups with negative expectancy.
Early signal
Drawdown DNA
What causes your losing streaks
What is Drawdown DNA?
Analyses the sequence of events that leads to your worst losing periods. Identifies trigger trades, escalation patterns and the point of maximum damage — so you can break the cycle before it starts.
🎯 Most drawdowns follow the same pattern. Knowing yours lets you stop it at step 1.
Early signal
Monte Carlo simulation
Statistical confidence in your edge
What is Monte Carlo simulation?
Runs 500 simulations of your trading by randomly sampling from your trade history. Shows the range of possible monthly outcomes and calculates the probability that your edge is real — not just luck. The distribution chart shows most likely outcomes in green, unlikely losses in red.
🎯 Below 80% confidence = keep logging. Above 80% = your edge is statistically real.
